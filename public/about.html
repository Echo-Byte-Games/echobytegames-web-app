OUR STORY

HELLO, WE'RE ECHO BYTE

Echo Byte Games is an independent game studio built on a simple idea: games should feel like a little gift. We love the kind of game you can pick up for five minutes and end up playing for an hour.

We are a small team, which means everyone has a hand in everything. From the first sketch on a napkin to the final polish pass, we make decisions together and care about every detail that reaches a player.

Our worlds are made of pixels because we believe limits spark creativity. A tiny sprite, a snappy soundtrack and a clever mechanic can take you further than you might think.
